About

I am an analytical, motivated undergraduate studying Forensic Science (Honours with Research) at Lovely Professional University, Punjab. My work brings together scientific rigour, research methods and laboratory management.

I want to contribute to India’s government forensic infrastructure, with a particular interest in institutions such as the Central Forensic Science Laboratory (CFSL), Kolkata under the Directorate of Forensic Science Services, Ministry of Home Affairs. My long-term aim is to master laboratory management, research validation and forensic administration, and to lead a forensic laboratory.
